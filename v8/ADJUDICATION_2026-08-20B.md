## 1. CANDELS Tolman K2 — executed: NO KILL FIRES; systematics-dominated

Primary (early types, registered bins): μ̄_med is **non-monotonic**
(19.97 → 20.49 → 20.42 → 20.06) and the registered linear fit fails its
own goodness-of-fit: n = −0.03 ± 0.27 with **χ² = 13.1 on 2 dof** — a
value > 3σ from every registered prediction *including* n = 0-adjacent
nonsense, which is the signature of an invalid estimator, not physics.
Registered robustness variants scatter across 2.5 units of n
(faint-quartile −0.36; narrow-mass +0.58 ± 0.42; Kormendy-adjusted
+2.18 ± 0.18 with the only acceptable χ²): the μ̄ statistic is
**population/selection-confounded at a level that swamps the forecast
precision** (σ ≈ 0.1 forecast; ≈ 1 realized — the third over-optimistic
power forecast of the campaign, logged as such). Per the registration:
K2-T-select does not fire; the Kormendy-controlled variant (the one
built for exactly this confound) lands on A′'s 2.2 but is a robustness
lane and is NOT promoted. Band-spread statistic: **S = −1.51 ± 0.93** —
consistent with 0 (artifact) and with the model's −0.03; 2.3σ from the
"L&S-spread-is-real" extrapolation (+0.6). *Leans artifact,
non-decisive.* The pure angular-shape statistic remains the cleaner
instrument; the SB statistic adds little at this sample size.

## 2. Flux-selected angular rerun (required item) — shape survives, weaker

Without any FLRW mass cut (brightest 40% per bin, N = 822 early types):

## 3. Concessions from the external critique (adopted)

1. **The tautology.** θ = R/D_A: only the ratio is observed. Given the
   measured shape, ANY distance law is matched by evolution
   ∝ D_A·ln(1+z) — βs = −1.00 for coasting is exact algebra *because*
   the measured shape is 1/ln(1+z), not because Euclidean geometry was
   "caught." The identity demonstrates observational indistinguishability
   on this sample, nothing more. Precedent: López-Corredoira (2010) —
   to be cited; no novelty claim.
2. **The parsimony grant was unfair.** "Zero shape freedom" handed the
   static model "no size evolution" free, while V8's own
   evolving-contents adoption (SFR density × ~10 over this z range)
   makes frozen radii a strong untested prior. Symmetric grading charges
   it. **The K2-ang ledger row is downgraded** to: *"angular sizes:
   consistent with Euclidean D_A + zero size evolution; exactly
   degenerate with FLRW + βs ≈ −1; frame-independent discriminator not
   yet run."*
3. **The coupled-tests point.** The angular pass and the Tolman R-band
   fail share one D_A: the static distance that makes sizes
   "non-evolving" is the same one that makes the R-band galaxies
   under-dimmed on the local SB–R relation. They are two faces of one
   choice and are now presented as such — not tallied one win, one loss.
4. **RETRACTION (prior-wavelength error).** The symmetric-grading claim
   "expansion fails its own Tolman test at −4.2σ (R)" used the 0.40z
   FP/M_L prior — a rest-B quantity — against a rest-3600 Å
   measurement where BC passive evolution is ~1 mag. The prior was
   wavelength-wrong; the −4.2σ number is withdrawn (the qualitative
   floated-vs-committed point stands). **The same error invalidates
   both Branch-E verdicts** (the static I-band flip to +3.2σ and the
   R-band rescue): all Branch-E numbers, in both directions, are marked
   unreliable pending per-rest-wavelength evolution priors from an
   actual population model.

## 4. The frame-free path (adopted as the program's next instruments)

- **σ–M★ dynamical test (pre-registration to follow before any σ data
  are touched):** velocity dispersions are frame-free; at z ≈ 1.5–2 the
  static D_A is ~2.2× ΛCDM's while d_L (hence M★) differs by ≲ 20%
  (the Hubble-degeneracy sector), so the static frame requires
  M_dyn/M★ ≈ 2× the ΛCDM-frame value on the same published compact
  quiescent samples (van de Sande 2013; Belli 2014+). The registered
  output is the required M_dyn/M★(z) under each frame against the local
  ratio; the reading implications are declared in the registration, not
  after.
- **Comoving number-density of compact massive ETGs** (local census vs
  z ≈ 2 under the static volume element): second frame-breaking
  handle; to be scoped.
- These, not more θ(z) or μ̄(z) fits, are what can break the
  D_A ⊗ evolution degeneracy. The mainstream read of βs ≈ −1 rests on
  exactly these two legs; engaging them is the honest cost of the
  Euclidean reading.
