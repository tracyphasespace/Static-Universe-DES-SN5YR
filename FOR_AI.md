## What is this?

A **specified, falsifiable non-expanding-universe model** tested against
the Dark Energy Survey's 5-year Type Ia supernova release (DES-SN5YR),
with all code, raw data, pre-registrations, and adverse results public.
Version 8.0 (DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22032094](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22032094))
is the human-circulation freeze.

The model: static Euclidean space with **evolving contents** (not the
old eternal steady state), plus two photon–vacuum interaction channels —
an achromatic forward drag that produces redshift with D = (c/K)ln(1+z),
and a chromatic (λ^−1/2) non-forward channel that produces the Hubble-
diagram curvature usually attributed to dark energy. Light-curve time
dilation comes from a direction-resolved transient response of the
medium ("wake"), giving Δt_obs = Δt_em(1+z)^κ_r with κ_r ≈ 1 measured.

## Do I care? (the two-sentence version)

On the released DES-SN5YR distance vector, this static model fits within
**Δχ² = +1.1 of flat ΛCDM** with one shape parameter on each side — and
it survives the classical objection that killed tired-light models
(it *predicts* time dilation rather than lacking it). The claim is
**not** "the universe is static"; it is that **the SN Hubble diagram
alone no longer uniquely demonstrates accelerating expansion** — the
data are consistent with either geometry or propagation physics, and
orthogonal tests must decide.
