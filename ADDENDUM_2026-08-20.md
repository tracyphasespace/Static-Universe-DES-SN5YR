## 3. The geometric sector: everything sharp turned out degenerate

The measured early-type angular-size shape is θ ∝ 1/ln(1+z) (robust to
removing the FLRW mass selection), but this is **exactly degenerate**
with FLRW plus size evolution βs ≈ −1, by the algebra θ = R/D_A
(only the ratio is observed; cf. López-Corredoira 2010). The earlier
"K2-ang fired for Euclidean" claim is **downgraded** accordingly. The
Tolman kills and rescues are all evolution-prior-dominated (and two of
our own numbers were **retracted** for a wavelength-mismatched
evolution prior: the "expansion fails at −4.2σ" figure and both
Branch-E sub-verdicts). Structural conclusion, stated plainly: **flux
and geometry cannot decide the static-vs-expanding question on these
data.** The D-M model-reading decision is therefore gated on
frame-breaking observables (next item).

## 6. Standing rule adopted: forecast honesty

Three consecutive ~10× optimistic power forecasts (T4, K2, σ(b₂))
share a cause: error budgets omitted intrinsic population scatter and
